## 📋 Step-by-Step Guide for Sellers

### Getting Started (5 minutes)

**Step 1: Login**
1. Use `seller@test.com` / `seller123`
2. You'll see the dashboard

**Step 2: Explore Existing Products**
1. Click **"Products"** in left sidebar
2. You'll see 2 sample products
3. Try switching between Grid and List view
4. Click Edit icon to see product details

**Step 3: Add Your First Product**
1. Click **"Add Product"** button
2. Fill in the form:
   - **SKU**: Unique product code (e.g., TECH-002)
   - **Name**: Product name (e.g., Bluetooth Speaker)
   - **Category**: Choose from Electronics, Clothing, Home & Garden
   - **Brand**: Choose from TechPro, StyleCo, HomeMax
   - **Unit Price**: Selling price (e.g., 49.99)
   - **Cost Price**: Your cost (e.g., 25.00) - optional
   - **Stock**: Available quantity (e.g., 50)
   - **Min Order**: Minimum quantity buyer must order (e.g., 1)
   - **Unit**: unit, piece, box, kg, etc.
   - **Visibility**: Toggle on to show to buyers
   - **Active**: Toggle on to enable selling
3. Click "Add Product"

**Step 4: Bulk Import Products (Optional)**
1. Click **"Import Products"** button
2. Click **"Download Template"** to get CSV format
3. Open template in Excel/Sheets
4. Fill in your products (see template format)
5. Save as CSV
6. Click "Choose File" and upload
7. Click "Import Products"

**Step 5: Onboard Your First Buyer**
1. Click **"Buyers"** in left sidebar
2. Click **"Onboard Buyer"** button
3. Fill in buyer details:
   - First Name: John
   - Last Name: Smith
   - Email: john@company.com (they'll use this to login)
   - Business Name: ABC Corp
   - Credit Terms: 30 days (or 0/60/90)
   - Credit Limit: 10000
4. Click "Onboard Buyer"
5. **Note down the generated password** to give to your buyer

**Step 6: Set Custom Discounts for Buyer**
1. Find the buyer in the list
2. Click the **Edit icon** next to their name
3. Choose discount type:
   - **Category**: e.g., 10% off all Electronics
   - **Brand**: e.g., 15% off all TechPro products
   - **Product**: e.g., 20% off specific product
4. Click "Add Discount"

## 💡 Tips

1. **CSV Import Format**: Download the template first - it shows exactly what fields are needed
2. **SKU Must Be Unique**: Each product needs a unique SKU code
3. **Stock Management**: Set realistic stock quantities - they'll decrease when buyers order
4. **Min Order Quantity**: Set this if you have minimum order requirements (e.g., buy at least 10 units)
5. **Credit Terms**:
   - 0 days = Immediate payment required
   - 30/60/90 days = Buyer gets that many days to pay after order
6. **Discounts Stack**: Category + Brand + Product discounts will all apply if set
7. **Visibility Toggle**: Turn off visibility to hide products from buyers temporarily
